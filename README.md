# TASK-18: KYC and Wallet Activation Explainer

A single-page explainer resolving five recurring points of KYC confusion for Redbelly Network: when KYC is required, the ten-wallet-per-identity limit, typical approval wait time, current regional restrictions, and KYC as a staking prerequisite.

## Sourcing summary

Three of five points are cited to an official Redbelly source (mainnet-vs-wrapped KYC requirement, current restricted-jurisdiction list, KYC-for-staking). The staking point is now backed directly by the developer portal's User access and Staking pages rather than whitepaper inference alone. Two points (ten-wallet limit, 3 to 5 minute approval time) are reported firsthand by the contributor, with informal corroboration from a Redbelly moderator in the Discord support channel, but no screenshot or message link on file. They are marked `[COMMUNITY-REPORTED, UNCONFIRMED, NO PUBLISHED SOURCE]` throughout, since no published official document states either figure and the Discord corroboration is not independently verifiable without that evidence.

One discrepancy against the task brief is flagged rather than silently corrected: the brief assumes regional restrictions have been removed. Redbelly's live Terms and Conditions (Clause 15) still list 18 restricted jurisdictions with no indication of a reduction from a prior list. The deliverable states the current list as verified fact and does not claim that residency outside the list guarantees KYC approval, since the Terms do not state that either way.
