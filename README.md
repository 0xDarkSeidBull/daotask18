# TASK-18: KYC and Wallet Activation Explainer

A single-page explainer resolving five recurring points of KYC confusion for Redbelly Network: when KYC is required, the ten-wallet-per-identity limit, typical approval wait time, current regional restrictions, and KYC as a staking prerequisite.

## Sourcing summary

Four of five points are cited to an official Redbelly source (mainnet-vs-wrapped KYC requirement, current restricted-jurisdiction list, KYC-for-staking, and the wrapped-token existence claim). The staking point is backed directly by the developer portal's User access and Staking pages rather than whitepaper inference alone. The wrapped-token point is backed by Redbelly Network's own official X account confirming both Ethereum (ERC-20) and Solana (SPL) deployments, rather than inference alone; the token standard on any chain beyond these two remains unchecked. Two points (ten-wallet limit, 3 to 5 minute approval time, the latter now framed as a community estimate rather than a general "most submissions" claim) are reported firsthand by the contributor, with corroboration from two named Redbelly moderators, Appie and Daniel Bressoud, in the Discord support channel. Screenshots and direct message links for both are included alongside each claim. They are marked `[COMMUNITY-REPORTED, UNCONFIRMED, NO PUBLISHED SOURCE]` throughout, since no published official document states either figure.

One discrepancy against the task brief is flagged rather than silently corrected: the brief assumes regional restrictions have been removed. Redbelly's live Terms and Conditions (Clause 15) still list 18 restricted jurisdictions with no indication of a reduction from a prior list. The deliverable states the current list as verified fact and does not claim that residency outside the list guarantees KYC approval, since the Terms do not state that either way.
